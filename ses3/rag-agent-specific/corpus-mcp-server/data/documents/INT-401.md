# INT-401: NovaCure Internal Toxicology Memo TOX-2026-014: NCX-7401 90-day rat chronic toxicity update

- source_id: `novacure_internal_docs`
- source_type: `internal_report`
- date: 2026-06-01

Internal memo, NovaCure Preclinical Safety group. 90-day chronic dosing in rats at 3 dose levels found no dose-limiting toxicity at the intended clinical dose range. Mild, reversible elevation in liver enzymes observed at the highest dose only. Recommends continued monitoring of the hERG signal flagged in the published literature (see LIT-103) during any further clinical dosing.
