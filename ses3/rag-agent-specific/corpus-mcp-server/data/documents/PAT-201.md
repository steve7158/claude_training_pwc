# PAT-201: US Patent 11,987,234 - Composition of matter: NX-14 receptor antagonist compounds

- source_id: `uspto_patent_full_text`
- source_type: `patent`
- date: 2024-02-20

Assignee: NovaCure Therapeutics, Inc. Claims a genus of small-molecule compounds, including NCX-7401 (Example 14), that selectively antagonize the NX-14 receptor. Specification describes in vitro binding assays (Ki < 50 nM for NCX-7401) but does not disclose in vivo efficacy or safety data. Patent term extends to 2044.
