# LIT-103: Off-target cardiac ion channel activity observed across the NX-14 antagonist chemical class

- source_id: `pubmed_central`
- source_type: `literature`
- date: 2026-01-14

Journal of Cardiac Pharmacology, Vol. 12. Screening of six NX-14 receptor antagonists, including NCX-7401, against a hERG ion channel panel found measurable off-target inhibition (IC50 range 1.8-6.4 uM) across the class. Clinical relevance at therapeutic exposure is not yet established, but authors recommend cardiac safety monitoring in any human trial of this chemical class, including QT-interval assessment.
